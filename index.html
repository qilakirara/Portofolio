Thesis research

Bachelor’s thesis · Andalas University · 2026 · Supervisor: Dr. Syafrizal, S.E., M.E.

The Effect of Store Image on Customer Satisfaction through Perceived Value at Citra Swalayan Andalas Branch, Padang

Pengaruh Store Image terhadap Customer Satisfaction melalui Perceived Value pada Citra Swalayan Cabang Andalas Padang

A quantitative explanatory study of how a store’s image shapes customer satisfaction, directly and through perceived value. It applies the S-O-R framework, Signaling Theory, and Expectation-Disconfirmation Theory, with questionnaire data from 153 respondents analyzed in PLS-SEM (SmartPLS 4). All four hypotheses were supported, with partial mediation.

Respondents (n = 153)

Female 68%
Age 31–40: 32%
Students 39.9%
Shop 2–3×/month: 52.9%

Method. Purposive sampling (minimum 80 under the 10-times rule; 153 collected) via Google Forms with Likert scales; measurement and structural models evaluated with bootstrapping and PLSpredict.

Key findings

  • Store image has a strong effect on perceived value (f² = 0.672) and a smaller direct effect on satisfaction (f² = 0.099).
  • Perceived value partly explains how a good store image becomes satisfaction (indirect β = 0.268).
  • Highest-rated item: complete product selection (mean 3.92 of 5).

Implications and recommendations

  • Keep the product range and friendly, helpful staff; improve comfort, cleanliness, and uncrowded aisles.
  • Treat store improvements as a way to raise value perceived by customers, not only sales.
  • Future research: add service quality, customer experience, trust, and price fairness; use other retail types and a longitudinal design.

Limitations

  • R² values are in the weak range (0.25–0.49), so other factors also matter.
  • One store only, cross-sectional data, and self-reported questionnaires.
